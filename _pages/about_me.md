I am Balthazar Courvoisier, a quantitative researcher at Queensfield AI and a PhD student working on deep learning for finance in collaboration with PSL's LAMSADE laboratory. My work connects statistical modeling, machine learning, and systematic investment strategies.

Trained at ENSAE and Polytechnique. My experience spans market-neutral strategy development, fixed-income risk controls, and financial forecasting. Alongside my research, I teach Python for Finance in the Master’s in Asset Management at Dauphine PSL.

## Experience in quantitative finance

- **Quantitative Researcher at [Queensfield AI](https://www.queensfield.ai/about-us.html)** · Since January 2024
  - Implemented a market-neutral alpha strategy based on pairs trading.
  - Pursuing PhD research on deep learning for finance in collaboration with PSL LAMSADE.

- **Quantitative Research Intern at [Ostrum Asset Management](https://www.ostrum.com/fr/ostrum-en-bref)** · 2023, six months
  - Investigated Hurst exponents across a range of underlying assets for low-frequency trading research.
  - Developed and deployed a stop-loss and take-profit mechanism for fixed-income desk strategies.

- **Quantitative Research Intern at [Crédit Agricole CIB](https://www.ca-cib.fr/nous-connaitre)** · 2022, six months
  - Modeled and forecast financial performance for long-term planning using econometric methods.
  - Implemented OLS, LASSO, and SARIMAX models, with a systematic feature selection methodology for multivariate data.

- **Data Science Intern at [BNP Paribas Cardif](https://www.cardif.fr/partenaires/qui-sommes-nous)** · 2021–2022, six months
  - Developed an econometric robustness index to assess smartphone durability.
  - Processed large datasets with Python in a cloud environment, using NLP and clustering to clean and enrich the data.

- **Data Science Intern at [Agence France Locale](https://www.agence-france-locale.fr/a-propos-de-lafl/)** · 2021, three months
  - Used clustering to identify financial trajectories of French municipalities and examined their relationship with social factors.
  - Implemented statistical metrics for ongoing reporting.
